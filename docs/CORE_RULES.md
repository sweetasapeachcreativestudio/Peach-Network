# Peach Network — Locked MVP Rules

## Business
- Businesses may join immediately.
- Memberships issue Peach Coins monthly.
- One-time Peach Packs are available without membership.
- Membership rollover is capped by plan.
- Coins are service credits, not cash and not transferable.

## Creative admission
- Creatives apply.
- Peach reviews portfolio and experience.
- Peach assigns level.
- Creatives cannot self-select Peach level.

## Levels
1. Peach Seed
2. Peach Sapling
3. Peach Tree
4. Peach Blossom
5. Peach Root

## Matching
- No public bid wall.
- Peach privately matches qualified creatives.
- Match logic may use specialty, level, portfolio, availability, location and reliability.
- Offers expire after 72 hours if unanswered.

## Offer
Creative sees:
- scope
- deadline
- revision rounds
- exact cash payout
- accept / counter / decline

## Coins
- Available: customer can spend
- Held: committed to an accepted project
- Spent: project approved/completed
- Released: returned from a cancelled/unaccepted project

## Projects
Recommended stages:
- Matched
- Offer Accepted
- In Progress
- Proof Uploaded
- Revisions
- Approved
- Completed

## Progress protection
- Creatives submit progress updates.
- Updates can include screenshots/files.
- No update for a configured period can create an admin alert.
- “Waiting on Client” prevents the creative from being punished for client-caused delays.

## Deadline handling
If overdue:
- Continue with Creative
- Extend deadline
- Request cancellation
- Contact Peach Support

## Disputes
Initial disputes are manually reviewed by Peach.
AI should not automatically decide refunds or payouts in MVP.

## Payout
Business approval does not mean the business pays the creative directly.
Peach approves the creative payout.
Stripe Connect handles connected creative payout accounts.

## Finance
Track separately:
- Gross sales
- Tax collected
- Stripe/payment fees
- Creative payouts
- Refunds
- Outstanding Peach Coins
- Peach contribution margin

Tax collected is not Peach income.

## Profit guardrail
Normal projects should target roughly 35–40%+ contribution margin after direct creative payout and direct transaction costs.
Low-margin projects require admin review.
